# Code of Conduct

> Fictional document created for the OrbitDesk demo. Orbit Systems is not a real company.

## 1. Our Commitment

Orbit Systems expects every employee, contractor and board member to act with integrity, respect and fairness. This Code applies at work, at company events and online when representing Orbit.

## 2. Respect and Inclusion

### 2.1 Equal Treatment

Decisions about hiring, pay, promotion and assignments must be based on merit and must never consider gender, age, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation or any other protected characteristic.

### 2.2 Harassment

Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated. Report any incident to your manager, your HR Business Partner, or anonymously through the OrbitSpeakUp hotline.

## 3. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality with a value of up to USD 50 per supplier per year. Anything above USD 50 must be declared in the Gifts Register within 5 working days and approved by your manager. Cash or cash equivalents (such as gift cards) must never be accepted.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

Declare any outside employment, board seat, or investment in a supplier or competitor to HR before it starts. Employees must not manage a close family member.

## 5. Confidential Information

Company and customer information must only be shared on a need-to-know basis. Do not paste confidential information into unapproved AI tools or personal email.

## 6. Speaking Up

### 6.1 How to Report

Concerns can be raised with your manager, HR, the Ethics Committee (ethics@orbit.example) or the anonymous OrbitSpeakUp hotline, which is available 24 hours a day.

### 6.2 No Retaliation

Orbit Systems strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Retaliation is itself a breach of this Code.

## 7. Consequences

Breaches of this Code may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. All employees must complete Code of Conduct training every year and acknowledge this Code in OrbitLearn.
