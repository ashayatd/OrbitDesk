# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

> Fictional document created for the OrbitDesk demo. Orbit Systems is not a real company.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees travelling on company business or spending money on behalf of Orbit Systems. All business travel must be pre-approved by your line manager in the OrbitTravel tool.

## 2. Booking Travel

### 2.1 Flights

Book flights through OrbitTravel at least 14 days before departure where possible. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires approval from a Vice President.

### 2.2 Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is USD 180 in standard cities and USD 260 in high-cost cities (London, New York, San Francisco, Zurich, Singapore, Tokyo). Hotels must be booked through OrbitTravel.

### 2.3 Ground Transport

Use public transport or standard ride-hailing. Rental cars need manager approval. Personal car use is reimbursed at USD 0.60 per mile.

## 3. Meals and Per Diem

The daily meal allowance is USD 75 per day for domestic travel and USD 95 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## 4. Submitting Expenses

### 4.1 Deadline

Expense claims must be submitted in OrbitExpense within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 90 days are rejected.

### 4.2 Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense above USD 25. Lost receipts require a signed missing-receipt declaration, allowed at most twice per year.

### 4.3 Approval and Payment

Your manager approves claims within 5 working days. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

## 5. Corporate Card

Employees who travel more than 4 times per year may apply for an Orbit corporate card. Personal expenses must never be charged to the corporate card; any personal charge must be repaid within 10 days.

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Items

Not reimbursable: fines and penalties, minibar charges, personal entertainment, upgrades without approval, and travel insurance (Orbit already provides travel insurance through its global policy).
