## 1. Purpose

This document defines the 2026 salary bands used by HR for offers, promotions and annual reviews. It must not be shared with employees or managers outside HR.

## 4. Rules for Offers

New-hire offers must not exceed the **band midpoint** without approval from the **VP People**. Offers above the band maximum are not allowed.

## 5. Promotion Increases

A promotion increase is normally **8% to 12%** of base salary, and the new salary must be at least the minimum of the new band.

## 6. Annual Review Budget

The 2026 merit increase budget is **3.5%** of total payroll per department.
