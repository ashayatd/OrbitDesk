# Leave Policy

> Fictional document created for the OrbitDesk demo. Orbit Systems is not a real company.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains the types of paid and unpaid leave available to all permanent employees of Orbit Systems. Contractors are covered by their agency agreement and are not eligible for the leave types below unless their contract says so.

## 2. Annual Leave

### 2.1 Entitlement

Every permanent employee receives **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Employees who join mid-year receive a pro-rated entitlement of 2 days per full month remaining in the year.

### 2.2 Requesting Annual Leave

Annual leave must be requested in the OrbitHR portal at least **5 working days in advance** for absences of up to 3 days, and at least **15 working days in advance** for absences longer than 3 days. Your line manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days.

### 2.3 Carry-over

Up to **5 unused annual leave days** may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**; any carried-over days not used by then expire. Days above the 5-day limit expire on 31 December and are not paid out.

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 days of paid sick leave** per year. For absences of more than **2 consecutive days**, a medical certificate must be uploaded to OrbitHR within 3 days of returning to work. Sick leave does not carry over.

## 4. Parental Leave

### 4.1 Primary Caregiver

The primary caregiver receives **26 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, which can start up to 2 weeks before the expected date of birth or adoption.

### 4.2 Secondary Caregiver

The secondary caregiver receives **4 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption. It may be split into at most two blocks.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

Employees receive **5 days of paid bereavement leave** for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling) and **2 days** for other relatives.

## 6. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to **30 days** per year may be approved by your manager and the HR Business Partner once annual leave is exhausted. Health benefits continue during unpaid leave.

## 7. Public Holidays

Orbit Systems observes **10 public holidays** per year, published in OrbitHR every December. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off within 30 days.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy go to the HR helpdesk (hr-help@orbit.example) or your HR Business Partner.
