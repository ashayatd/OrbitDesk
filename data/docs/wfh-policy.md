# Work From Home Policy

> Fictional document created for the OrbitDesk demo. Orbit Systems is not a real company.

## 1. Eligibility

All employees who have completed their **90-day probation period** may work from home. Roles that require on-site hardware access (for example data-centre technicians) are excluded unless their manager approves an exception.

## 2. Hybrid Schedule

### 2.1 Standard Arrangement

The standard arrangement is **hybrid: at least 2 days per week in the office** (Tuesday and Thursday are team anchor days). Employees may work from home on the remaining days.

### 2.2 Fully Remote Exceptions

Fully remote work requires written approval from your department head and HR. Approvals are reviewed every **6 months**.

## 3. Working From Another Country

Employees may work from outside their country of employment for up to **20 working days per calendar year**, subject to manager approval and a tax check by HR submitted at least **10 working days before travel**. Working from a sanctioned country is never permitted.

## 4. Equipment and Allowance

### 4.1 Home Office Allowance

Eligible employees receive a one-time **home office allowance of USD 400** for a desk, chair or monitor, claimed through the expense system within 6 months of becoming eligible.

### 4.2 Internet Allowance

A monthly **internet allowance of USD 30** is paid with salary to employees who work from home at least 2 days per week.

### 4.3 Company Equipment

Company laptops must not be used by family members or connected to public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

## 5. Security Requirements

When working from home you must lock your screen when away, use the company VPN for all internal systems, and never print confidential documents on a home printer.

## 6. Availability

Employees working from home must be reachable on chat during **core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 local time**.
