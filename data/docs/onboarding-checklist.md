# New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

> Fictional document created for the OrbitDesk demo. Orbit Systems is not a real company.

## 1. Before Day 1

- HR sends the welcome email with your **employee ID** and start time.
- IT ships or prepares your laptop; it is handed over at the IT desk on Day 1.
- Your manager assigns an **onboarding buddy**.

## 2. Day 1

- Collect your laptop and badge from the IT desk (2nd floor) at **09:30**.
- Set up **OrbitAuth MFA** with IT before leaving the desk.
- Complete the **"Welcome to Orbit"** session at 11:00.

## 3. Week 1

- Complete the mandatory **Security Awareness** training in OrbitLearn.
- Complete **Code of Conduct** training and acknowledge the policy.
- Set up the VPN (see the VPN Setup Guide) and test it from home.
- Add your bank details and emergency contact in OrbitHR.

## 4. First 30 Days

- Agree on 30/60/90-day goals with your manager.
- Complete **Data Protection** training in OrbitLearn.
- Meet your HR Business Partner.

## 5. Probation

The probation period is **90 days**. Your manager holds a probation review in week 12. Some benefits (for example working from home) start only after probation.

## 6. Mandatory Training Deadlines

All mandatory trainings (Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, Data Protection) must be completed within **30 days** of joining. Incomplete training blocks access to production systems.
